Senguard Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Senguard Discord bot ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Bot on Discord.

By using the Bot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Server Data
- Server ID and server name
- Channel IDs (for tickets, logs, welcome messages)
- Role IDs (for staff roles and permissions)
- Server configuration settings (which features are enabled)

### 1.2 User Data
- User IDs (for applications, warnings, tickets)
- Usernames (for display purposes)
- Application answers (only if you submit a staff application)
- Moderation records (warnings, mutes, bans issued via the Bot)

### 1.3 Message Content
The Bot processes message content **temporarily** to detect spam, profanity, or rule violations (AutoMod) and to execute prefix commands such as `-ping`.

**We do not permanently store your messages.** Messages are processed in real-time and discarded immediately after moderation checks are completed.

### 1.4 What We Do NOT Collect
- Passwords or login credentials
- Payment or financial information
- Private messages (DMs), unless you initiate an application or support conversation
- Voice data or audio recordings
- Personal information such as real names, addresses, or phone numbers

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide the Bot's features (moderation, tickets, applications, welcome messages)
- Enforce server rules through AutoMod and anti-raid protections
- Improve the Bot's performance and fix bugs
- Respond to support requests
- Prevent abuse and malicious activity

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored securely on our servers using reasonable security measures.
- We take precautions to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.
- No system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We **do not sell, trade, or rent** your data to third parties.

We may share data only when:
- Required by law or legal process
- Necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Bot, its users, or the public
- You have given explicit consent

---

## 5. Data Retention

- Server configuration data is retained as long as the Bot remains in your server.
- User data (warnings, applications, tickets) is retained for up to **90 days** after it is no longer needed.
- When the Bot is removed from a server, its configuration data is deleted within 30 days.

---

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data — request a copy of the data we hold about you
- **Delete** your data — request deletion of your data at any time
- **Opt-out** — stop using the Bot to prevent further data collection

To exercise these rights, contact us via our support server.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under 13 years of age. If you are under 13, please do not use the Bot. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.
